# E-Commerce Business Analytics & Machine Learning Project

## About the Project

This is an end-to-end E-Commerce Business Analytics and Machine Learning project that I worked on to understand how data can help businesses make better decisions.
The main aim of this project is to analyse e-commerce data, understand business performance, identify customer behaviour, and use machine learning to predict future business outcomes.
I used Power BI to create interactive dashboards and Python to perform data processing and machine learning.
The project includes **10 dashboard pages**, covering both business analysis and machine learning predictions.

## Dashboards Included

| Page | Dashboard Name | What It Shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Executive Overview | Overall business performance at a glance |
| 2 | Sales & Profitability | Sales, profit, and profit margin analysis |
| 3 | Customer Analytics | Customer behaviour and purchasing patterns |
| 4 | Product Analytics | Product sales, performance, and ratings |
| 5 | Marketing Analytics | Discounts, coupon usage, and marketing performance |
| 6 | Operations & Delivery | Delivery time, delays, and on-time delivery |
| 7 | Returns & Customer Experience | Product returns, customer ratings, and feedback |
| 8 | Sales Prediction | Comparison of actual sales and predicted sales |
| 9 | Return Prediction | Identifying orders with a possibility of being returned |
| 10 | Customer Churn Prediction | Identifying customers who may stop purchasing |

## Technologies Used

- **Power BI:** Created interactive dashboards and reports.
- **Python:** Used for data processing and machine learning.
- **Pandas:** Used to clean and analyse datasets.
- **NumPy:** Used for numerical operations.
- **LightGBM:** Used for machine learning predictions.
- **Excel / CSV:** Used for handling datasets.

## Machine Learning Work

Along with business analysis, I worked on three prediction areas:

**1. Sales Prediction**

Used historical sales data to generate predicted sales values. The objective is to understand future sales trends and support business planning.

**2. Return Prediction**

Built a prediction workflow to identify orders that have a higher possibility of being returned.

**3. Customer Churn Prediction**

Analysed customer-related data to identify customers who may stop purchasing from the business. This can help businesses focus on customer retention.
